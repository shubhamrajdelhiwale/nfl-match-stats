Quarterback play is another major factor in the Denver Broncos vs Los Angeles Rams contest. Efficient passing can keep an offense ahead of the chains and create more options on third down.

Key Factors Across These NFL Matchups

Although each matchup is different, several statistical categories are useful when analyzing NFL games. Quarterback efficiency, rushing production, defensive pressure, turnovers, third-down conversion rate and red-zone performance can all influence how a game develops.

Quarterback Efficiency

Completion percentage, passing yards, touchdown passes, interceptions and third-down efficiency help show how effectively an offense moves the football.

Running Game

A productive rushing attack can help control the clock and create balance. It can also reduce pressure on the passing game and create opportunities through play-action.

Defensive Pressure

Sacks, quarterback hits, tackles for loss and forced turnovers can reveal how effectively a defense disrupts an opposing offense.

Turnovers

Turnover margin is one of the most closely watched statistics in football. Interceptions and fumbles can produce short fields and additional possessions.

Red-Zone Performance

Teams need to convert offensive opportunities into points. Red-zone touchdown percentage can help explain differences in scoring efficiency.
